A NOTE FROM OUR TEAM

Letter from the Secretary-General

Dear Delegates,

On behalf of the Secretariat of Hope Model United Nations, it is my distinct honour and pleasure to invite you to join us for the first session of HopeMUN, a conference where we aim to underscore the importance of diplomacy, dialogue and international cooperation, alongside providing an opportunity for the participants to explore networking opportunities all around the world as well as forming new friendships. Most importantly, we aim to utilise the power of our community to support those who need it most.

HopeMUN is a conference where the delegates will have the chance to learn and discuss the world's most vital topics of discussion by actively taking part in it, effectively coming to an understanding of international relations, the scope of different countries, and world history. Only through dialogue and cooperation can we make this world a better place for all the people living in it, without discrimination. As MUN'ers of today are the leaders of tomorrow, we hope to see all kinds of innovative thinking and creativity of young minds to come up with new perspectives and solution ideas. We, the youth, are the Hope for a better world, and these simulations are a way of seeing how much we can achieve in future. I hope our guiding light will contribute to the flame that rests in all of your chests.

I assure all participants that the time they will spend on HopeMUN will be worth it. I am more than eager to welcome you all.

Best regards,
Yağız Eren Şahin
Secretary General of HopeMUN'26

Letter from the Director-General

Dear participants,

It is my great honour and pleasure to invite you to the first edition of HopeMUN'26. I am proud to serve as Director General of a conference that was born from a cause, not just an idea.

HopeMUN'26 is committed to Ata, who is struggling with Spinal Muscular Atrophy (SMA), a rare genetic condition that is both extremely costly and time-sensitive, and is striving to gain access to gene therapy, which is not yet covered by the government in our country. Our aim is to raise awareness of SMA across the MUN community and to use the support of our delegates to launch a fundraising campaign for this child's treatment. Every speech delivered, every committee session held and every new friendship formed here will carry a much deeper meaning, extending far beyond the conference itself.

A conference of this kind cannot take place without the support of those who believe in it. I would like to express my sincere gratitude to our Secretary General, Yağız Eren Şahin, and to our hard-working organisation team and heads, who have given their time and energy to this conference from the first day.

As the Executive Team, we aim to create an environment where academic development and social interaction complement one another, and where delegates from different backgrounds can learn from one another. Above all, we want each of you to know, as you leave HopeMUN'26, that your participation has made a real difference to a child's life.

We cannot wait to welcome you and to see what we can achieve together.

Best regards,
Şakir Fuat Aksu
Director General, HopeMUN'26

OUR PURPOSE01 / 04

More than a conference.

We use the reach of the MUN community to make SMA visible and give hope to a child.

HopeMUN is a volunteer initiative raising awareness of spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) through Model United Nations. We want MUN's reach to serve those who need it most. Every bit of conference revenue is dedicated to supporting Ata's treatment.
